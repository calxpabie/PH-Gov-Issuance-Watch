Issuances LGU HR should know

  • 2026 Year-End Bonus and ₱5,000 cash gift — DBM says to pay with the first November 2026 payroll. Bonus = one month’s basic pay as of Oct 31; needs at least 120 days of service from Jan 1 to Oct 31 and being in service on Oct 31. Source ↗
  • Administrative Order No. 43 (3 Jun 2026) — optional SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG deductions for contract-of-service and job-order workers. Binds national agencies; LGUs are only encouraged to follow. Source ↗
  • Executive Order No. 103 (6 Nov 2025) — full devolution deadlines: cities 2027, provinces and municipalities 2028. Source ↗
